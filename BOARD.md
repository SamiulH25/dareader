Keep it small: one ticket = one shippable change + acceptance line.
IDs are free-form (`APP-06`, `RUNTIME-07`, …); top of `Todo` = highest priority.

```
### XXX-00 — title
- Files: path/to/file.kt
- Want: what changes, one line.
- Accept: how to verify (command, behavior).
```
